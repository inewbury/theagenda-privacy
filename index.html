The Agenda

Privacy Policy

Effective May 7, 2026

At a glance

All data is stored locally on your device

No data is transmitted to any server

No third-party analytics or tracking

No account or sign-up required

What data the app stores

The Agenda stores workout sessions, nutrition logs, body weight entries, streak history, and profile information. All of this data is saved using on-device storage (UserDefaults) and never leaves your phone.

The home screen widget accesses a shared on-device container to display your workout and nutrition status. This data stays entirely on your device.

Data collection and sharing

The Agenda does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app makes zero network requests. There are no servers, no cloud storage, and no external services of any kind.

Third-party services

The Agenda uses no third-party frameworks, SDKs, or services. It is built entirely with Apple's first-party frameworks: SwiftUI, Charts, WidgetKit, and UserNotifications.

Notifications

The app may send local notifications to remind you about workouts or nutrition. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device and do not involve any external push notification service.

Data deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting the app. No data will remain on any server because none was ever sent to one.

Children's privacy

The Agenda does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Because the app does not collect data, there is no way to notify users directly of changes.

Contact
