Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026 · Version 2026-11

At a glance

We collect health data only from you, in the app, and only with your consent

We never sell or share it. Only our hosting processor stores it for us.

See, export or delete it, or withdraw your consent, at any time

About this policy

This policy covers “consumer health data” as defined by Washington's My Health My Data Act, collected by The Agenda: Strength (“the app”), made by Isaiah Newbury (“we”, “us”). It adds to our Privacy Policy, which covers everything else the app does with your data.

The health data we collect

  • Body measurements: age, sex, height, weight, and body weight entries over time.
  • Goals and activity: your goal (lose, maintain or gain weight) and your activity level.
  • Exercise: workout sessions, with the exercises, sets, reps and weights you log, how long each session took, any notes you write, the program days you customize, and your place in your program.
  • Nutrition: daily calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat.
  • Consent records: your answers about health data backup and analytics, and when you gave them.

We don't collect location data, biometric data, reproductive or sexual health data, or data from Apple Health, wearables or other apps.

Where it comes from

Only from you: everything above is what you enter in the app. We don't buy health data or get it from anyone else.

Why we collect it and how we use it

  • To back up and sync your training across your devices, so it's safe if you lose or replace your iPhone. This is the purpose you consent to when you create an account.
  • Only if you turn on Analytics, a separate consent: aggregated analysis of workout, nutrition and body data to improve the programs. It never includes your name or your notes.

We don't use your health data for advertising, and we don't sell it.

Who we share it with

We don't share your health data with third parties or affiliates. It's stored for us by our processor, Supabase, Inc. (database and sign-in hosting, in the United States), which processes it only to provide that service to us, under a Data Processing Addendum. We would disclose it only if the law requires it.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Confirm and access: confirm whether we collect, share or sell your health data, and get a copy of it. Settings → Privacy → Export My Data gives you a copy at any time.
  • Know who receives it: get a list of the third parties and affiliates we've shared it with. Today the only one that handles it is our processor, Supabase, Inc.
  • Delete it: Settings → Privacy → Delete Account deletes your account and all its health data from our servers and from your iPhone.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off health data backup or Analytics in Settings → Privacy. Backup stops; what's already backed up stays in your account until you delete it.

You can also email theagenda.support@gmail.com with any of these requests. We may ask you to confirm the request from your account, to make sure it comes from you. We'll respond within 45 days of receiving your request. If we need more time, we may take up to 45 more days; if so, we'll tell you, and why, within the first 45 days. Requests are free.

When you ask us to delete your health data, we delete it within 30 days of verifying your request, and we tell everyone we've shared it with to delete it too. Today that's only our processor, Supabase, Inc. Our hosting provider, Supabase, makes routine backups as part of its service; we don't make or keep our own copies of your data. Deleted data can stay in those backups until Supabase's routine schedule replaces them.

Appeals

If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision, or by emailing theagenda.support@gmail.com with “Appeal” in the subject line. We'll respond to your appeal in writing, with the reasons for our decision, within 45 days of receiving it. If we deny your appeal, you can file a complaint with the Washington State Attorney General.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new version number and effective date. We won't collect new kinds of health data, or use or share it for new purposes, without asking for your consent first.
